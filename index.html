Why Yuzu

Classic logic games, with someone to play them with

Board games are better with company. Yuzu thinks, barks his move, sulks when he loses and waves when he wins.

He plays back

Against Yuzu he is the other player: Four in a Row, Flip, Bowls, Last Biscuit, Yuzu's Secret and ten more. On Hard he looks up to nine moves ahead.

Or he sits beside you

With Yuzu you reason out a puzzle while he sniffs, cheers and sleeps: Bone Hunt, Bedtime, Toy Boxes, Paw Picture, Yarn Sort and nine more.

Easy that teaches

Every game has three levels. Easy shows his threats, rules out impossible squares and says whether a move is good. Hard does not.

A look inside

Fifteen against him, fourteen with him

How it works

Pick a game, pick a level, play

  1. Choose a side

    The front page has two tabs: the games against Yuzu, and the games with him. Every game explains itself the first time you open it.

  2. Choose how hard

    One, two or three paws. After two wins in a row Yuzu offers a harder game; after three losses, an easier one.

  3. Walk him home

    Every win earns blue buttons. Twelve take Yuzu to the next place, from the park all the way home.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Yuzu free?

Yes. All 29 games and every level are free. Yuzu Plus is an optional one-time purchase that removes the ads between games.

When do ads appear?

Only between games, never during one, and not in your first games. You can also choose to watch a short video for one more hint or to double the blue buttons.

Is it for children?

Yuzu is made for everyone who likes logic games. The games are classics that adults play, with a friendly dog.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Only the ads need a connection.

How do I restore Yuzu Plus?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore a purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Yuzu comes to iPhone first.
